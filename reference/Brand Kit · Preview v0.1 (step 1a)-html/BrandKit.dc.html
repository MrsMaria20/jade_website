06 · ASCII DOVES

Flight, in type.

The signature visual from Field & Flight: doves made of characters flying over the blurred field. It'll ship as a self-contained canvas component, along with the field background.

LIVE · MOVE YOUR CURSOR THROUGH THE FLOCK
Drawn in type

Each dove is built from JetBrains Mono "·" and "•" characters in Porcelain #F4F1E8, with soft motion trails.

Depth

Seven birds at different sizes. Closer birds are larger, brighter, and faster.

Interactive

Birds gently veer away from the cursor and loop back once they leave the frame.

Calm by default

With “reduce motion” on, the flock is drawn once as a still image. Animation pauses off screen.

07 · VOICE AND MOTION

How it speaks and moves.

The behavior behind the look, so the built site feels like the mockups, not just looks like them.

VOICE
Calm, clear, quietly confident

Plain words over buzzwords. Say what we do in the first line.

Specific over grand

“Designed and built in [X] weeks” beats “world-class design.”

Honest about AI

Crafted by a person, accelerated by AI. Never overpromise what isn't offered yet.

Key lines

“Built to carry you further.” · “Today, we build websites. Next, we'll help them think.”

MOTION
Drift, don't bounce

Slow eases (cubic-bezier .2,.7,.15,1), 1–1.3s reveals. Nothing snaps.

Headlines rise

Lines slide up from behind a mask, one after another.

Scroll tells a story

The bloom section pins and grows with scroll, built with GSAP ScrollTrigger.

Respect reduce motion

Every effect has a still version for people who turn motion off.
