01 · HOW A PROJECT GROWS
I — ROOTS · WEEK 1 We start below the surface. A conversation about your business and customers, then a written plan you can hold us to. YOU RECEIVE → one-page plan · sitemap · timeline
II — GROWTH · WEEKS 2–4 Then we design and build. You see the design before any code is written. Once you sign off, we build it fast, accessible, and easy to update. YOU RECEIVE → design · two revision rounds · preview link
III — BLOOM · LAUNCH You launch, and keep growing. We connect your domain, hand over every account, and stay on hand after launch while real customers arrive. YOU RECEIVE → live site · every login · care guide
