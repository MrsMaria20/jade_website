# Voice and copy

## Who we're talking to

Small to medium businesses of every kind: shops, studios, professional services, hospitality, trades, wellness. The size is the focus, not the industry. Write for a broad audience; don't single out one industry.

## How we sound

Results first. Say what the business gets, not how the work is done.

- **Lead with the outcome.** A brand that resonates with the people it wants to reach. A site that does its job. A simple process that ends with a live website in [X] weeks.
- **Plain and direct.** Short sentences, concrete claims. Fewer adjectives, no atmospheric language.
- **No buzzwords.** No "cutting-edge," "seamless," "leverage," "unlock," "empower."
- **Specific over grand.** "Live in [X] weeks" beats "world-class design."
- **Don't explain the method.** Skip how the work is built, what tools are used, and where AI helps. The client should never have to think about building a site or learning a site builder.
- **Honest about AI services.** AI assistants and agentic workflows are "coming soon," never implied as available now. Mention them only where those services are discussed.
- **Honest about being new.** No invented stats, reviews, client names, or years in business. Concept work is always labeled as concept work.
- **Timelines are ranges you can keep.** Use the owner's real experience (about 4 to 7 days for a small to medium site, 2 to 3 weeks for a very detailed one), and say when the clock starts.
- **Questions are real ones.** Only publish FAQ entries for questions that have actually been asked.
- **We** on most of the site. **I** is fine on an About section if one is added later.

## Key lines

- Hero: **"Websites built to represent you."**
- Hero blurb: **"Custom websites for small to medium businesses, built so your brand resonates with the people you want to reach. We define what your site needs together, then handle every step from there. In [X] weeks, your site is live."**
- AI bridge: **"Coming soon: AI assistants that handle the routine work, so you can focus on what matters most. Email us to hear first."**
- Call card: **"20 minutes."** Don't mention cost, even though the intro call is free.

## Mechanics

- Sentence case for headings and buttons.
- Buttons are verbs: "Book a call," "Start a project," "See our services."
- Placeholders stay in [BRACKETS] until real content exists: [X] WEEKS.
- No prices, revision rounds, or change-request rules on the site. Those are settled on the call and in the written agreement.
